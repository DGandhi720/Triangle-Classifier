"We run a stained-glass studio. Customers enter three side lengths and we tell them the shape and whether it’s buildable. If it’s not a valid triangle, show a message, don’t crash. Also flag right-angle pieces — they need reinforced corners. It has to be correct every time; a wrong answer means a ruined pane."


## CustomerRequirements
1. The first part of the program involves determining if the three sides, inputted by customers are able to be built. 
2. If they don't meet the requirements or satisfy what's needed for the program, the program shouldn't produce a crash, but rather just produce a message.
3. Right Angle pieces have to be flagged, as they require special necessities. If they aren't, then its equivalent to a ruined pane.
